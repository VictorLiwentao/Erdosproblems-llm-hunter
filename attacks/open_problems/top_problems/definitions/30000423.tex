\section{Recognition and Characterization of Leaf Powers}\label{30000423}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 30000423 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A graph is a $k$-leaf power if there is a tree whose leaves are its vertices and two vertices are adjacent exactly when their distance in the tree is at most $k$. Such a tree is a $k$-leaf root.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each $k\geq5$, characterize $k$-leaf powers and determine the complexity of recognizing them. \sref{30000423}{2}
