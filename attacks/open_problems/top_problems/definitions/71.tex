\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


An $n$-qubit pure state is a unit vector in $(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes n}$, considered up to global phase. Quantum gates act by the permitted unitary maps, and measurements produce classical outcomes according to the chosen measurement model. Entanglement means that a joint state cannot be represented as a product of subsystem states. Quantum error correction encodes logical information in a larger state space to protect it against a specified error class. A computation or control task must additionally specify its inputs, outputs and resources.
