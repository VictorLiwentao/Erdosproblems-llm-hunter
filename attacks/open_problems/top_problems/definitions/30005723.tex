\section{Modular Generators for Massive Double Cones}\label{30005723}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30005723 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


For a von Neumann algebra with cyclic separating vacuum vector, modular theory supplies a one-parameter group of automorphisms and its generator. A double cone is the intersection of the causal future of one point with the causal past of another. $M_-$ denotes the particular modular-generator component used in the report; multiplication means pointwise action in the specified representation.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For a massive free scalar field localized in a double cone, determine whether the modular-generator component $M_-$ is independent of the mass and whether $M_-$ is a multiplication operator.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The decomposition defining $M_-$ is source-specific. Mass independence of this component would not automatically imply mass independence of the entire modular flow.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine whether the double-cone modular-generator component for a massive scalar field is mass independent and acts by multiplication.
