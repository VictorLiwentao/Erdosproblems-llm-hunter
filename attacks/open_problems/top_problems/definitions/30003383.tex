\section{Strong-Interaction Limits of the Hohenberg–Kohn Functional}\label{30003383}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30003383 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
The Hohenberg--Kohn or Levy--Lieb constrained-search functional minimizes kinetic plus interaction energy among quantum states with prescribed one-particle density. In the strong-interaction limit the leading interaction problem is multimarginal optimal transport, with every marginal determined by that density. A Monge solution is concentrated on the graph of deterministic maps from one particle position to the others. The zero-point correction describes the next contribution from small quantum fluctuations about the leading optimal configurations. Bose or Fermi statistics constrain the symmetry of the underlying quantum state.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For the Hohenberg–Kohn functional and its multimarginal optimal-transport limit, determine the strong-interaction asymptotics; prove the zero-point-energy correction in one and, if possible, three dimensions; describe corrections due to quantum statistics; establish deterministic Monge solutions in three dimensions with more than two marginals; and construct effective approximate transport maps or functional derivatives for density-functional theory.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
All five tasks are retained, including the one-dimensional zero-point correction and the expressly conditional aspiration in three dimensions. Establishing the leading transport limit alone does not settle these next-order, statistics, Monge and approximation questions.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Understand the strong-interaction density-functional limit, its quantum corrections, three-dimensional transport maps and usable functional approximations.
