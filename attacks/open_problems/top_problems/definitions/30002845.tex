\section{Constructive Selection for Bessel Sequences}\label{30002845}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 30002845 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A Bessel sequence with bound one satisfies $\sum_j|\langle x,u_j\rangle|^2\leq\lVert x\rVert^2$ for every $x$. $u_j^*$ is conjugate transpose, $u_ju_j^*$ is a rank-one positive-semidefinite matrix, and the matrix norm is operator norm.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $\{u_j\}_{j\in J}\subset\mathbb{C}^n$ be a finite Bessel sequence with bound $1$ and $\lVert u_j\rVert^2\leq\varepsilon$. Given scalars $t_j\in[0,1]$, design a constructive algorithm that finds $J^\prime\subset J$ satisfying $\left\lVert\sum_{j\in J^\prime}u_ju_j^*-\sum_{j\in J}t_ju_ju_j^*\right\rVert=O(\varepsilon^{1/8})$. \sref{30002845}{2}
