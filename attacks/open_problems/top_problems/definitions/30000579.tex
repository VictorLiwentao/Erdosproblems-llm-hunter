\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} Baroclinic instability transfers potential energy from a stratified, vertically sheared mean flow into growing perturbations. Linear theory predicts growth rates and spatial structures by linearizing about that mean state. Oceanic eddy statistics include their horizontal scales and amplitudes; the deformation scale reflects the balance between stratification and rotation.
