\section{New Quantum LDPC Codes with Good Parameters}\label{30006387}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30006387 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A family of quantum codes has parameters $[[n,k,d]]$: number of physical qubits, number of encoded qubits and minimum weight of a nontrivial logical operation. Low-density parity-check means a bounded check weight and bounded number of checks incident on each qubit. Asymptotically good means both $k/n$ and $d/n$ stay bounded away from zero along a growing family.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Construct new families of quantum low-density parity-check codes with asymptotically good parameters.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}Asymptotically good quantum LDPC families already exist. The source asks for new families or constructions, rather than the historical existence question.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct additional quantum LDPC code families with positive asymptotic rate and relative distance.
