\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
In the mixed-characteristic-polynomial optimization problem, maximize the largest zero subject to $u_i$ lying in the hyperbolicity cone, $\operatorname{tr}(u_i)\leq\varepsilon$, and $\sum_i u_i=e$.
