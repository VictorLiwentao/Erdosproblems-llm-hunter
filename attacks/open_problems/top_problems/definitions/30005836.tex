\section{Dissipation and Interface Misorientation in Phase-Field Plasticity}\label{30005836}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30005836 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A phase-field variable describes a diffuse interface. Stored energy is recoverable from the state, whereas dissipation is irreversible loss during evolution. Crystal misorientation compares local lattice orientations; sharp-interface calibration compares diffuse-interface predictions with limiting interface motion. Energetic degeneracy means different microstructures have equal energy.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
How can energy storage be separated from dissipation in plasticity, crystal misorientation represented at phase-field interfaces, sharp-interface processes calibrated, dissipation in discrete phase transformations quantified, and energetically degenerate microstructures distinguished computationally?

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}This is a bundled set of modelling and computational questions, not one already specified phase-field PDE.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Separate storage and dissipation, represent interface misorientation, calibrate sharp-interface limits, quantify transformation losses and distinguish equally energetic microstructures.
