\section{Two-Categorical Interpretation of a Quantum Lattice Model}\label{30006634}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30006634 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A two-category has objects, morphisms and morphisms between morphisms, with two compatible compositions. In a quantum lattice model, defects and their junctions can furnish categorical symmetry data. The report refers to its explicitly computed two-dimensional lattice-model example, rather than to every two-dimensional Hamiltonian.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Give a categorical interpretation of the computed symmetry structure in the two-dimensional quantum lattice model, and determine whether it is naturally governed by a two-category.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The exact lattice model is the one referenced by the source contribution. Supplying an unrelated lattice model would change the target.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Identify the categorical structure underlying the computed lattice symmetry and decide whether it requires a two-category.
