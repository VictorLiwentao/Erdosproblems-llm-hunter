The main steps of the reconstruction are (i) to derive a complete distance matrix using shortest-path distance on the weighted contact network, (ii) to use a result of distance geometry based on the three dominant eigenvectors of the metric matrix (yielding the Gram matrix of the structure) (iii) or approximating the 3D structure using multidimensional scaling techniques.
