\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $Q_1,Q_2,Q_3$ be quadratic forms in $n$ variables, with $Q_1$ nonnegative on the variety $Q_2=Q_3=0$. Find the least degree $2d$ for which there are homogeneous polynomials $P,R_2,R_3$ of degree $2d$, with $P$ a sum of squares, such that $PQ_1+R_2Q_2+R_3Q_3$ is a sum of squares. Can $d$ be bounded independently of $n$? \sref{30006212}{2}
